# Terms of Service

**Last updated: 18 September 2026**

These terms apply to the private, read-only TikTok analytics connector maintained by Eduard Epshteyn.

## Service
The connector lets an authorized TikTok account owner read profile information and public video performance data through TikTok's official APIs. It does not publish, delete, comment, message, or otherwise modify TikTok content.

## Authorization
You must authorize the connector through TikTok's OAuth flow. You are responsible for the TikTok account and permissions you authorize. You may revoke access through TikTok at any time.

## Availability
The connector is provided as-is for personal use. TikTok API availability, scopes, rate limits, and permissions may change.
